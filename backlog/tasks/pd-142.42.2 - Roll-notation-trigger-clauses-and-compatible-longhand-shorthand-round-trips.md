One internal roll model with two spellings: every existing shorthand (adv/dis, kh/kl/dh/dl, r, Nx, result table, NATURAL labels) has an exact :trigger(action) longhand, and longhand is a superset (conditions on the total, bounded recursive reroll, (null) to cancel defaults).
