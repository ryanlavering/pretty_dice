## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
An attack set can chain a damage roll: :hit(2d8+2), :crit(+2d8). 'Hit' needs a target: optional 'vs N' on the roll (later $ac) or a Hit → roll damage button on the log entry because the DM declares the hit. Chained rolls are logged with their parent and kept together in Copy/Save, which also answers 'which roll counts' (PD-142.14.1.16). Origin: the DM never rolled damage for three goblin hits in Playtest 2. Design: docs/design/roll-triggers.md (draft, 2026-11-14). Origin: owner review of Playtest 2 (PD-142.14.2.1).
