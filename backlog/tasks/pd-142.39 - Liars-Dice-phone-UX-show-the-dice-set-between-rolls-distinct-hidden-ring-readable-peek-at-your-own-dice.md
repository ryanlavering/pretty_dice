## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Owner feedback 2026-11-14 on the phone artifact (screenshots: docs/design/screenshots/2026-11-14-liars-dice-phone-1.png and -2.png).

1. The stage is mostly empty while it is your turn to roll, or while the bot thinks. If the stage has to be blank between rolls, show the Showcase view of the dice set about to be thrown instead of an empty mat (reuse the showcase presentation, no new buttons).
2. Hidden-dice rings are confusing: after a roll the dice vanish (they are under the cup) but the same gold rings stay behind. Gold means HOLD everywhere else (RING_COLORS), so here it reads as 'held / leave out'. Use a distinct ring colour for 'hidden under the cup' (add it to RING_COLORS; never reuse gold or the cocked red-magenta), and make it obvious that the dice exist but are hidden (e.g. a cup/lid glyph or dim ring). Consider a clearer 'N of M' cue for how many dice each player has.
3. Your own roll is tiny in the player row (three small pip icons) and hard to read, yet the info matters for every bid. Needs a larger 'peek' of your own dice that is easy to bring up (for old eyes) and easy to hide from casual sight on a shared device: e.g. press-and-hold on your row (or the strip) shows big dice for as long as you hold, hidden on release. NO new top-level button or Roll button (PD-142.38 rule).
4. Check the same hidden/peek behaviour on the pass-the-device screens.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
