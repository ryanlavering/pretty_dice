## Description

Owner (v25, #resetview): Reset view sometimes leaves the dice cocked, mostly with sets.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Cause: resetView() only reset the camera (orbit, zoom). A die turned by hand or by the spin (rig quaternion, or each mesh in a set), and since .38.35/.38.38 poses carried over a rebuild, stayed cocked. Fix: in the Design showcase it also resets the rig and every die quaternion and the spin velocity. In Play the dice lie as thrown (camera only). The spec fails without the fix.

Owner resolved the artifact thread (resolve = ok), 2026-11-18.
