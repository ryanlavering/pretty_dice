## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Owner review of PD-142.1 on 2026-11-13.

1. BUG: hold rings disappear after a re-roll. They must re-show once the new roll settles (and redraw correctly on every state change: bumps after a re-roll, die tipping, release, new set).
2. Held dice are currently welded in place (static bodies). That preserves the face but breaks physical immersion. Instead let a re-roll bump held dice (they may slide or tip over). Their held state AND face of record are preserved like any settled die; when the roll settles, restore the face of record by a nudge or lift-spin-set-down. Coordinate with PD-125 (magic nudge-back when a re-roll bumps an established roll) and PD-63 (tap to nudge/reroll unsettled dice): one shared restore mechanism.
3. Precedence: lean trumps hold. A hold cannot be placed on a leaning (cocked) die. If a held die ends up leaning after a bump, show the cocked ring (red-magenta), and it needs to settle or be restored before the hold ring returns.
4. Ring redraw correctness is part of acceptance: add tests for re-roll, bump, tip-over and restore (extend tests/hold.spec.mjs).
