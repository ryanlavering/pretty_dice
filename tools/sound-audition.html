PD-114 · dice sound system

Dice Sound Audition

Single impacts cut from real CC0 recordings. Listen to each one, keep the clean clacks and drop the rest, then send me the picks. The auto-sorting into "dice on dice" and "dice on mat" is a guess from brightness, so correct it by ear.

Throw it

Plays a throw the way the app would. Every clip starts the instant it is scheduled, so the gaps between hits come from a small bounce simulation: each die rebounds at a fraction of its impact speed and waits as long as that flight takes, then tumbles to a stop. Dice also knock into each other. The app picks a clip recorded about as hard as the impact it is playing. The slider blends the two sets: Per hit picks one set for each hit in that proportion, Layered plays every hit from both at once. Everything goes through a limiter, and the peak readout shows what it let through.

Output peak: play a roll to measure.

Clips

Clack is measured from each clip: high-frequency share, brightness, short decay, little low body. 0 is a dull thud, 1 is a sharp click.
