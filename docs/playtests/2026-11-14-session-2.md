## What worked (matches the guide)
- Group roll: `3x d20+4` is accepted (rewritten `3x 1d20+4`); results read side by side with a per-roll breakdown in the table pill and the log.
- Initiative tracker: '+ init' adds a row per group roll; Next moves the highlight.
- Advantage: `d20 adv+5` becomes `2d20kh1+5`; the kept die shows plainly, the dropped die in brackets.
- Result tables work once saved: the '→ line' shows under the total and in the log (the DM could not read the clipboard to check Copy).
- Quick roll bar and saved sets (including after a browser restart); 'Who is rolling?' names later rolls; '+ label' works; held chip ('N held · release') matches the guide.
- On the phone, once dice settle the total and breakdown chip are readable with nothing overlapping (better than round 1).

## Notes and caveats
- The sandbox ran at load average 20-47 with up to four software-GL Chromiums (about 350% CPU each, and closing a role's browser did not stop its processes: the driver `close` only detaches). Rolls took 60-90 s, several clicks and screenshots timed out, and Mira lost her first turn. Findings tied to timeouts are marked unverified. Excluded as environment, as in round 1: slow rolls, the 'UI paused ... tap to copy details' toast (several agents called it debug text shown to players; same class as round 1's exclusion), the total appearing late, and the 1280x600 screenshot size.
- Budget: about 240k went on the first (setup) round with four fresh agents; continuing the same agents with `SendMessage` was far cheaper per turn. The 75k check-in was done with the owner via the integrator relay; the integrator then asked to continue to budget.
- Driver note: kill a role's browser by its `--user-data-dir` profile path when done; `pw.mjs close` leaves the process running.
- Agents have no ground truth for roll fairness (as in round 1); results are reported, not verified.
- Cards are labelled `auto-playtest`: findings came from AI agents against build 9bfde69. Re-check at v4 head before fixing.
