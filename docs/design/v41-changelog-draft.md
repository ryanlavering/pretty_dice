# 4.1 changelog (draft, user-facing wording)

> Superseded: the final wording is in CHANGELOG.md (4.1 section).

- **Yacht: no more accidental scores (PD-156, PD-165, PD-172).** On a touch screen, tapping a box on the scorecard now only previews it; double tap (or long press, your choice under the scorecard) to enter the score. With a mouse a single click scores. After a score there is a 3-second "Whoopsie! Undo" button with a countdown that puts back your dice, rolls and turn; after 2 seconds the score is locked in. Computer players wait for the window to close.
- **Change a die's design during a game (PD-158).** In a running game you can now pick another design from the Bag, a set with the same dice, or press Randomize: only the look changes. Dice, their values and holds stay as they are. A design or set with different dice (another shape or count) is still refused, with a message.
- **Craps: bet while a computer player shoots (PD-159).** When a bot is the shooter it places its bets, then waits. Place or change your bets (choose who you bet as), then tap Ready and the bot rolls. Tables with only computer players play on by themselves.
- **Story Dice (PD-160).** A new game: roll a seeded set of glyph dice, arrange them in story order and rate the sentence they tell. After the roll the dice fly into two rows facing the camera (pool below, numbered rings above); the Roll log keeps each roll's story. Hi-vis keeps the flat tiles.
- **Removed: the Antique relief preset button.** The Patina and relief sliders still do the same by hand.
- **Story Dice polish (PD-171 to PD-176).** Dragged dice grow less and stay clear of their neighbours; the numbered rings and numbers sit behind the dice; the arrangement re-spreads when the screen resizes or you toggle full view, and uses one row on a landscape phone; the story sentence appears on the mat where the unplaced dice were, with the rating buttons in the dock, so you can play with the side pane closed; a bot's story always waits for you to grade it, even when only bots are playing.
- **Story Dice sets editor (PD-166).** Games > Story Dice > "Story Dice sets...": see, create, edit, copy, save and delete Story Dice sets (glyph pack or packs, number and size of dice, seed, same or different dice, shuffled or in pack order) with a live preview and the set text you can copy. Saved sets are ordinary bag sets.
- **Glyph dice in normal rolls (PD-167).** A roll formula can now deal glyph faces: for example `5d12{noto#1}` or `3d6{memes&noto#2}@bone`. The same text deals the same faces on every device. Faces count by their position in totals, so keep highest, advantage and the rest work on them too.
- **Safety net for a misread die (PD-155).** After dice settle, and again before a score is taken, the game re-checks that each die's value matches the face showing, and corrects it if not.
