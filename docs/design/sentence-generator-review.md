# Story sentence generator: review (2026-11-20)

Owner ask: "a review of the sentence generator. It's pretty good, but I think some medium-effort fiddling could make it much better." Medium pass on `src/story.js` (`sentenceFor`, `adLibFor`), branch `v4.1-sentgen`; no word-bank (pack) edits, exports and signatures unchanged.

Method: 418 stories (5 to 9 glyphs, every pack alone plus four mixes, salts 0 to 3, and 18 re-orderings of the same sets) generated before and after from the same inputs by a throwaway node script, read as a player would, then counted.

## Critique of the old generator

1. **Run-ons.** Connectors chained without limit: up to six clauses in one sentence ("the curious sky pointed, so the cloud covered the pail, so the golden daylight shone, so the penguin amused the old bird"). 24 of 418 stories had ", so" twice.
2. **Articles.** "a coins", "a footprints", "a scales", "a water", "an energy", "a hourglass": the a/an rule looked at the first letter only and knew nothing of plurals or mass nouns (21 hits).
3. **Echoes.** A glyph's noun met its own verb or adverb: "a smirk smirked", "the humbling loss lost", "an explosion exploded", "the trembling tear tearfully". 28 stories had a four-letter echo.
4. **Repetition.** Every pick was independent, so the same adjective or adverb came back in one story ("loudly ... loudly"), the same clause shape could run three times, and there were only 5 openers and 3 closers ("Far from here," on a fifth of all stories).
5. **Shape.** Almost every clause was "the ADJ NOUN VERBED the NOUN"; the three-noun list ("the pig, the eye and the dust rolled cheekily") was the dullest and most common 3-glyph shape. Non -ly adverbs landed mid-verb ("the hot flame high warmed the bird", "the twinned balance sideways balanced").
6. **No arc.** Ring order changed the words but not the story: the first glyph opened, nothing came back, a 9-glyph story was just three 3-glyph stories in a row. 5 vs 9 felt proportionate in length only.
7. **Sour/odd prepositions.** "inside" with people and animals ("waited inside the tired man", "a fossil roared inside the mysterious surprise").
8. **Ad-lib comma soup.** The bot's story was one sentence: aside then connector then aside ("dramatically, meanwhile, ... obviously, but also, ..."), 232 of 400 had an aside running straight into a connector. "Listen. the ..." left a lower-case letter after a full stop (39).
9. Fine already: determinism (seeded `rng`, no `Math.random`/`Date`), every glyph used once in ring order, word banks give a good picture-book flavour, the oddball packs (memes, hieroglyphs) are funny on their own.

## Changes (all in `src/story.js`)

1. **One word picker per story** (`words(r, lean)`): a glyph keeps one noun for the whole story, chosen so it does not echo its own verbs ("the smug face smirked"); adjectives, adverbs, verbs, prepositions, connectors and clause patterns are not reused within a story while a fresh choice exists; a word that shares a stem with the glyph's noun (or, for adjectives, its own verbs) is skipped; a 4-letter root used once is avoided later ("creaky door ... creaked"). An echo is preferred over nothing only when a glyph's bank has no alternative.
2. **Number-aware articles**: plural nouns (ending in s, not ss/us/is) and a short mass-noun list (water, sand, bread, energy, DNA...) take "some"; silent h (an hour); capitals read as letters (an L, a UFO, a 404).
3. **At most two clauses per sentence.** A clause either joins with a comma connector (and, but, so, then, until, while, or maybe; never one already in the sentence) or starts a new sentence, sometimes with a transition (Meanwhile, Then, After that, Just then, Later, Soon).
4. **New clause shapes**: "the cat that chased the moon purred beside the key", "while the cat purred, the moon warmed the key", "the cat chased the moon and purred", "the cat and the moon chased the key"; the three-noun list is gone; non -ly adverbs (sideways, far away, high) go after the verb phrase, -ly adverbs before or after.
5. **Openers 5 -> 10** (One stormy evening, Just before breakfast, At the edge of town, Without warning, Every Tuesday...), closers 3 -> 4; "inside" dropped from the prepositions, "past" and "around" added.
6. **A closing line for 8-9 glyph stories that calls back to the first die (the hero) and the last**: "The pixel would do it all again.", "And that is how the gem became so heavy.", "The dodo never forgave the whisker.", "The bicycle never spoke of the spell again.", "Nobody ever believed the crown." So the first and last ring now visibly matter. 6-7 glyph stories may get one short beat sentence ("Nobody saw that coming.", "This was not the plan.") for rhythm.
7. **Phone length budget** (`MAXLEN=200`): 8-9 glyph stories drop most adjectives, lean to 3-glyph clauses and skip transitions, and the closing line is added only when the story still fits about four lines on a 390 px phone (PD-184 test). Stories over 200 characters: 64 -> 30 of 418.
8. **Ad-lib**: same word picker and patterns; every clause after the first is its own sentence led by a connector ("Meanwhile, ...", "Long story short, ...", "Next thing you know, ..."), asides stay at the end of their clause, the "Listen." capital is fixed, a few new openers/connectors/asides (Fun fact:, True story:, on a Tuesday, to thunderous applause). Closing lines unchanged.

## Not done (ideas for later)

- **Word banks** are now the main source of oddness: generated packs give abstract or label nouns ("the hmm", "the owo", "the place of interest", "the pull", "a seal" for a wax seal) and odd verb-object pairs ("the chubby ice amused the telescope"). A pass that marks nouns as person/animal/thing (for who/which, "inside", "with") and drops label nouns would help more than further template work. That is a metadata change in `scripts/story-packs/` and the manifest.
- Single-verb glyphs still echo now and then ("the steamy steam", "opened the open book"): needs a second intransitive verb in those banks.
- Pronouns (it/they) and "who/which" need animacy; the generator uses "that" for now, which is always correct.
- A real arc (setup, trouble, twist) per salt, and dialogue lines ("'Not again,' said the cat") for the plain sentence.
- `hintFor` still shows the first two words of each kind; it could show the words the sentence actually used.
- Tone filter: verbs such as stabbed, killed, abducted, infected come from the fantasy and meme banks and can land on "the woman" or "the child" (hieroglyph pack). Not changed; see below.

## Owner taste wanted

1. **Closing line**: is the callback ("The moai never spoke of the grump again.", "And that is how the gem became so heavy.") the right voice for the plain prompt, or should the plain sentence stay neutral and leave jokes to the bot? Lines are in `CODA`.
2. **Short beats** for 6-7 glyphs ("Nobody saw that coming.", "This was not the plan."): keep, change or drop (`BEAT`).
3. **Length on phones**: the closing line is only added while a story stays under 200 characters (`MAXLEN`), so about 42% of 8-9 glyph stories get one, to keep the PD-184 four-line limit at 390 px. Raise `MAXLEN` (or allow five lines on a phone) if you would rather always have it.
4. **"Every Tuesday,"** and **"some coins / some water"**: fine as the house style?
5. **Dark verbs with people** (stabbed, killed, abducted, infected on man/woman/child in the hieroglyph pack): soften them in the banks, or keep as part of the fun?

## Tests

- New `tests/story-sentences-logic.spec.mjs` (fast-state, pure node, about 1 s): 300 seeded random arrangements over every pack and mixes, 5-9 glyphs: determinism, a/an by sound and letter name, no "a" before a plural, no double spaces or stray punctuation, every sentence capitalised, ends with a full stop, length 60-260 and 9-glyph stories longer than 5-glyph ones (but not runaway), reversed order always changes the story, the closing line names the first die, adjectives and adverbs do not repeat; the ad-lib gets the same hygiene and no aside piled into a connector.
- Existing specs (`story-dice`, `games-story`, `games-story-ui` incl. the PD-184 wrap test at three sizes, `story-sets-editor`, `grammar-roll`) pass unchanged; none hard-codes sentence text. `story dice: ?game=story opens the Games setup` timed out at 30 s twice inside the batch (fresh page boot under CPU load from another session) and passes alone with this change (21.5 s; 22.8 s on the old code). No golden file contains sentence text (checked `tests/golden/*.json`).
